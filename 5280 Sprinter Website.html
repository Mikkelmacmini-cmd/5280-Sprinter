01 // Your Sprinter Is Our Specialty

Everything your Sprinter needs, done right.

S-01 / HEALTH

Vehicle Health Check

Comprehensive bumper-to-bumper full-service health inspection for your Sprinter — detailed report with photos included.

S-02 / MAINT

A & B Service

Mercedes-Benz periodic maintenance programs. We'll consult to determine which service plan is right for your mileage and model year.

S-03 / BRAKE

Brake Service & Repair

Addresses brake warning lights with on-site replacement of worn pads, rotors, and sensors to keep you safely on the road.

S-04 / TRANS

Transmission Service

Colorado's steep mountain roads add serious strain on your drivetrain. Regular transmission service keeps your Sprinter reliable at altitude.

S-05 / DIAG

Diagnostics

Advanced diagnostics to identify and address any underlying issues — often diagnosable with a quick conversation before you even arrive.

S-06 / FLUID

Fluid & Routine Maintenance

Brake fluid, coolant, and power steering flushes alongside routine upkeep — filters, wiper blades, and everything in between.

We come to you.

Our mobile service unit brings expert Sprinter maintenance directly to your location — April 1 through October 31. Service A & B, oil changes, battery checks, inspections, and more. We participate in events throughout the greater Denver area, offering repairs and vehicle guidance on-site.
